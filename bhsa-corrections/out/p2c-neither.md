# Test B's class neither, read row by row (slice p2c)

Written by Claude (Opus 5.5) on 2026-11-13, New York time, as a sub-agent of the session that wrote this folder's README.md. Not reviewed by a human. Every form is a transcription's: the BHSA 2021's (`g_word_utf8`), MAM-simple's, or WLC 4.18's where it is named; nothing here says what any manuscript has.

The 169 rows are Test B's class neither (`out/35-part2-test-b-neither.txt`, `out/32-part2-scan.json`): BHSA words (slots) where MAM's pointing differs and the analysis (language, `lex`, `vs`, `vt`) is supported neither by the BHSA's own pointing elsewhere nor by the BHSA's words with MAM's pointing. A slot is one BHSA word, a part of an atom.

## Method

1. **Pre-filter** (`scripts/p2c_prefilter.py`, `out/p2c-prefilter.txt` and `.json`): for each row, every BHSA pointed key equal to the row's key after one small edit (a dagesh or mappiq added or removed, one vowel point removed, added or changed, a ḥolam moved to the next or previous letter, a shin dot exchanged for a sin dot or back; the review's key ignores accents, meteg and silluq, so an edit to U+05BD changes no key), with its analyses, counts and an example; the analyses of MAM's pointing in the BHSA; the spellings of the row's full analysis (with person, number, gender, state and suffix) elsewhere; and WLC 4.18's aligned atom with its notes. 14 rows have a one-edit neighbour analysed with another lexeme, stem or tense, and 7 have MAM's pointing in the BHSA with another analysis.
2. **Lookups** for the judgments: `scripts/p2c_lexforms.py` (`out/p2c-lexforms.txt`, the forms of 25 lexemes), `scripts/p2c_context.py` (`out/p2c-context.txt`, verses in the BHSA and MAM) and `scripts/p2c_slots.py` (`out/p2c-slots.txt`, the slots that reasons cite).
3. **Judgments**, by Hebrew morphology and the BHSA's own data, in `scripts/p2c_judgments.py`; this page and `out/p2c-neither.json` come from `scripts/p2c_table.py`.

## Categories

- **another-word**: the BHSA's pointing is a well-formed spelling of another attested word (another lexeme, or another stem, tense or person of the same lexeme), and the analysis departs from it. A correction candidate.
- **follows-pointing**: the analysis is what the BHSA's own pointing spells, even if rare, while MAM's pointing would have another analysis (Part 1's class a).
- **no-word**: the BHSA's pointing breaks a rule of Tiberian pointing: it lacks a vowel, a mappiq, a dagesh forte or a word-internal dagesh lene that the word needs, or it has a ḥataf vowel in a closed syllable or a mark where none can stand. The analysis reads the regular word through it, as at Genesis 34:11.
- **variant**: the BHSA's pointing is a regular or recognized spelling of the analysed word: a ḥataf vowel for a vocal shewa under a letter that is not a guttural (or MAM's ḥataf where the BHSA has the regular vowel), a full vowel for a ḥataf under a guttural, another vowel that spells no other word, a dagesh lene at a word's start (which depends on the atom before), a dagesh dirimens, the dot in a consonantal vav that the review's footnote `vav-dot` describes, a ketiv's letters with the qere's vowels where MAM has a ketiv and qere. Not a correction.
- **unsure**: the evidence does not settle the category.

- The BHSA's slot: מַּלְאֿכִים, in the atom הַמַּלְאֿכִים
- MAM's piece: מְּלָאכִים, in the atom הַמְּלָאכִים
- Analysis: `MLK/` 'king'; ps NA, nu pl, gn m, st a, prs absent; the lexeme's part of the slot in the ETCBC's segmentation (`g_lex_utf8`): מַּלְאֿך
- WLC 4.18's atom: הַמַּלְאֿכִים (notes 1)
- A one-edit neighbour (add a qamats on letter 3): מַּלְאָכִים, `ML>K/`, 17 times, as at Genesis 19:1
- Reason: The pointing of the mem and the lamed is that of ML>K/ 'messengers', which the BHSA has 17 times one vowel away, with qamats under the alef, as at Genesis 19:1, מַּלְאָכִים, while the alef here has no vowel, as in MAM's spelling of 'kings' with an alef; the analysis is MLK/ 'king', MAM's word (the review's class b case).
