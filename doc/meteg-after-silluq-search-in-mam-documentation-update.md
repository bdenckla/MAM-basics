## 1 Kings 7:37: Simanim means the Simanim Tanakh

Recorded by Codex on 2026-09-12. This entry corrects two passages: the 1 Kings 7:37 row of
finding 1 and the paragraph immediately after that table. Both passages say that MAM's note
identifies the Simanim Tiqqun. At both passages, “the Simanim Tiqqun” should read “the Simanim
Tanakh.”

The mirrored public introduction at `in/mam-ws-intro/appendices.mediawiki` defines `סימנים`,
under the list of editions based on the Aleppo Codex, as `תנ"ך סימנים (פלדהיים תשס"ח)`. The
introduction therefore identifies the edition directly; no inference about the Simanim Tiqqun's
haftarot is needed.
