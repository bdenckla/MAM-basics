# Koren lookup candidates for a meteg after the silluq in MAM

**What this list is for.** When this file was written, MAM had one known verse-final chanted word in which a meteg follows the silluq: 1 Kings 7:37, לְכֻלָּֽהְנָֽה׃ — the silluq is the U+05BD on letter 3 of 6, the meteg is the U+05BD on letter 5, and the stress is penultimate. This file ranks every other verse-final chanted word of MAM whose final atom has two U+05BD by how plausible the same reading is there, so that Ben can look the likeliest ones up in the Koren edition first. The reading being graded is called the **post-silluq reading** below: it takes the FIRST U+05BD of the final atom as the silluq, that is, it puts the stress on that syllable. Written 2026-09-09 by a Claude session (Task C of the meteg-after-silluq investigation) at MAM-basics commit `30fb7681`, from the tracked `gh-pages/MAM-with-doc/foi/foi-mtgmtg.json`, and revised the same day on the three decisions listed next. Ben has looked 4 of the 392 up in Koren so far, beginning 2026-09-09; the section "Koren readings so far" below lists them, the Koren column has each reading at its row, and the column is empty wherever he has not yet looked.

**Three decisions of Ben's, 2026-09-09, each of which overruled the first version of this file:**

1. **Syllables are counted the Masoretic way.** A full vowel or a shuruq is a syllable and a furtive pataḥ is a syllable of its own; a vocal sheva by either notation, a simple sheva or a hataf, is not a syllable but attaches to the syllable after it. The first version gave a hataf a syllable of its own and a furtive pataḥ none, and had a "borderline" verdict for one full vowel plus a hataf after the first mark. That verdict is gone: the 10 candidates that had it are plausible now, and no candidate changed class (measured by `.novc/mas_c_diff_revision.py` against the first version's analysis JSON, kept as `.novc/mas_c_analysis_before_2026-09-09-revision.json`).
2. **The basis of the syllable verdict is a measurement of Phonetic MAM, not a claim about Tiberian Hebrew.** The first version said that Tiberian stress is ultimate or penultimate; that sentence is withdrawn, and the section "Syllables after the stress in Phonetic MAM" below is what the verdict rests on.
3. **A column for the sheva after the first mark.** Phonetic MAM's resolution of that sheva, vocal or silent, together with whether the first mark's vowel is long; "long vowel + silent sheva" ranks first within a class. 1 Kings 7:37 is such a case, and it ranks 1.

**The calibration finding, and what a Koren reading means.** Ben confirmed on 2026-09-09 that Koren has לְכֻלָּֽהְנָה׃ at 1 Kings 7:37: one mark, on letter 3, the letter MAM's first U+05BD is on, against MAM's לְכֻלָּֽהְנָֽה׃. Koren has many metegs that MAM lacks, but where MAM has a meteg AFTER the silluq, Koren is expected to lack it. So for a candidate with two U+05BD:

1. Koren having ONE mark on the EARLIER letter is the signal — MAM's earlier mark is the silluq and the later one is a meteg after it, as at 1 Kings 7:37.
2. Koren having one mark on the LATER letter means MAM's earlier mark is an ordinary meteg before the stress.
3. Koren having both marks means MAM has no mark later than Koren's (Ben, 2026-09-10, at Psalms 35:10). Until that day this item said that both marks tell nothing.

## How to read the columns

1. **Rank** and **Class.** Class A: the post-silluq reading is plausible by the syllable count and no exact twin puts the stress on the last mark's syllable. Class B: plausible by the syllable count, but exact twins put the stress on the last mark's syllable. Class C: implausible by the syllable count. Within a class the order is: "long vowel + silent sheva" before the rest (Ben's decision, 2026-09-09); exact twins favouring the first mark, then none, then split, then favouring the last mark; suffix twins in the same four steps; then canonical order. 1 Kings 7:37 is the calibration case and lands at rank 1 in class A.
2. **Reference** is book chapter:verse in MAM's versification, and **Verse** says whether the verse is a prose verse or a poetic verse (`accgram.poetic_filter`; Job's prose frame is prose).
3. **MAM's form**, **First mark only** and **Last mark only.** The first is the chanted word as MAM has it, with its sof pasuq. The other two keep only the first, or only the last, U+05BD of the final atom, so that Koren's one-mark form can be matched by eye against them. In a maqaf compound (the Section 2 candidates) the earlier atoms are left exactly as MAM has them: a meteg on an earlier atom cannot be after the silluq. The forms keep their vowels because the vowels are what the syllable verdict and the sheva column rest on.
4. **Syllable verdict.** The post-silluq reading is graded by counting the syllables after the first U+05BD, the Masoretic way (Ben's decision, 2026-09-09): a full vowel or a shuruq is a syllable, a furtive pataḥ is a syllable of its own, and a vocal sheva by either notation — a simple sheva or a hataf — is not a syllable but attaches to the syllable after it. A holam male or a shuruq is written on a vav that is a mater and belongs to the consonant before that vav (the rule of `accgram.post_stress_meteg._nuclei`). The grading is relative to Phonetic MAM, per the section "Syllables after the stress in Phonetic MAM" below: there a verse-final stressed syllable is followed by at most one syllable, so two or more syllables after the first mark make the reading **implausible** — it has no attested parallel — and exactly one makes it **plausible** in principle. A first mark on a letter with a simple sheva, with a hataf, or with no vowel is implausible outright, since a silluq needs a full vowel under it.
5. **Sheva after the first mark** (Ben's decision, 2026-09-09). For a candidate whose first U+05BD is on a letter with a full vowel and whose next letter has a simple sheva, the cell names the vowel, says whether it is long, and says how Phonetic MAM resolves the sheva; otherwise it says that the next letter has no sheva, naming the vowel all the same, or that the first mark is not on a full vowel. Long: qamats (U+05B8), tsere, holam (U+05B9 or U+05BA), shuruq, and a hiriq followed by a yod with no vowel of its own (hiriq male); qamats qatan (U+05C7) and the other vowels count as short. Where the vowel is written on a mater — the vav of a holam male or a shuruq, the yod of a hiriq male — "next letter" is the letter after that mater. Phonetic MAM's `fva` field has U+05AF (HEBREW MARK MASORA CIRCLE) after every sheva it resolves as vocal; a sheva with a varika (MAM's mark for an implicit hataf) is vocal in Phonetic MAM's `jta` field with or without the circle; and a sheva with neither is silent. Checked over every chanted word of Phonetic MAM: 79,432 shevas with a circle or a varika (4 of them a varika with no circle), 79,432 vocal-sheva syllables in `jta`, equal. The heuristic, and its basis: a long vowel in a closed syllable of a lone atom is normally stressed, so "long vowel + silent sheva" argues that the first mark is the silluq. 1 Kings 7:37 is the case: in לְכֻלָּֽהְנָֽה׃ Phonetic MAM resolves the sheva of letter 4, the he after the first U+05BD, as silent (`jta` `l^.khul.lah.!na`), closing a syllable with a qamats that it does not stress, which is the configuration the post-silluq reading corrects. Figure 4 below is the base rate, which bears the heuristic out for every long vowel but the shuruq; within a class, "long vowel + silent sheva" ranks before the rest.
6. **Twin evidence.** The same atom elsewhere in MAM — same letters and points; accents, meteg, rafe, punctuation, CGJ and varika dropped, the join key of `py/tests/test_final_stress_vs_phonetic_mam.py` — with an impositive accent tells which syllable is stressed. "first" and "last" count the twins whose stressed syllable is the syllable of the candidate's first, or last, U+05BD, syllables being counted from the end of the atom the Masoretic way (item 4); a mark on a letter with no vowel of its own — a mater, or a letter with a simple sheva — is counted in the syllable of the nearest vowel before it, and a mark on a hataf letter in the syllable of the nearest full vowel after it, the syllable the hataf attaches to. "other" counts twins stressed on neither. Twins whose accent is written at the chanted word's edge rather than on its stress are not counted — pashta, telisha qetana, telisha gedola, segolta, tsinnor, tsinnorit, yetiv, deḥi and geresh muqdam (`final_stress.NOT_IMPOSITIVE`), and ole (this session's addition, 2026-09-09, because Phonetic MAM agrees with an ole's position only 13 times in 23). A verse-final twin is not counted as accent evidence either; but a verse-final twin with exactly one U+05BD has that mark as its silluq, so those are reported separately as "verse-final one-mark", and "verse-final two-mark" counts twins with the same two-mark shape, which say nothing. "atnach" repeats the exact count over the twins whose accent is an atnach, the pausal position nearest to a silluq. Where no exact twin exists, "suffix-3" (or, failing that, "suffix-2") reports the atoms sharing the candidate's last three (or two) letters with their points — weaker evidence, and reported as such.
7. **Twin example** and **Twin example's reference.** One twin lifted from MAM, from the majority side of the exact twins; where there is no exact twin, a suffix twin, and the reference cell says so and says which side it is on.
8. **Koren** is Ben's reading of the Koren edition, where he has made one — which of the two U+05BD on MAM's final atom Koren has, the first mark only, the last mark only or both, and the date he reported it — and is empty where he has not yet looked. The readings are tracked data, in `in/meteg_after_silluq_koren_readings.json`, and `.novc/mas_c_write_doc.py` fills the column from them.

The shuruq is different: Phonetic MAM resolves the sheva after the shuruq of a conjunctive vav as silent, as at Genesis 1:10 וּלְמִקְוֵ֥ה (`jta` `ul.mik.!vE`) — 3,589 unstressed silent against 114 unstressed vocal — so a shuruq before a sheva says nothing about the stress. Narrower still: a long vowel (shuruq aside) that has a U+05BD on it and a simple sheva after it is resolved vocal 537 times and silent 1 time, at Isaiah 66:3 מְבָ֣רֵֽךְ, whose U+05BD follows its munaḥ — a mid-verse meteg after the stress. So mid-verse a U+05BD on a long vowel before a silent sheva is all but unattested, and a verse-final first U+05BD in that position is unlikely to be an ordinary meteg. The full table by vowel, with examples, is in `.novc/mas_c_report.txt`.
5. **Exact twins favour the last mark or are absent; none favour the first.** Last 237, none 149, split 6, first 0. In all 6 split cases the minority twins are a merkha with the stress retracted before a following chanted word (checked: every minority twin is a merkha), which is evidence that does not carry to a verse end, where nothing follows. 100 candidates have a verse-final one-mark twin, and in 100 of them every such twin has its silluq on the last mark's syllable.
6. **The classes.** A 23, B 48, C 321. Every class A candidate is there for want of an exact twin, not because a twin favours the first mark.
7. **Phonetic MAM, as a cross-check of the counting.** Phonetic MAM (`MAM-private/al-hatorah/io/a01-phonetic-std-set`) takes the LAST U+05BD of a verse-final chanted word as the silluq, so for the candidates themselves it can only check that the last mark's syllable was counted right: it agrees on 392 of 392, with 0 unjoined. ## Syllables after the stress in Phonetic MAM, the basis of the syllable verdict

Over all 263,320 chanted words of Phonetic MAM (`C:/Users/BenDe/GitRepos/MAM-private/al-hatorah/io/a01-phonetic-std-set/*.json`, field `jta`, in which `!` precedes the stressed syllable, `.` separates syllables and `-` separates the atoms of a maqaf compound), the number of syllables after the stressed syllable within the stressed atom, counted the Masoretic way (a `jta` syllable holding `^`, a vocal sheva, or `8`, `6` or `0`, a hataf, is not a syllable; the syllable of a furtive pataḥ is one), is 0 for 209,262 chanted words, 1 for 54,046, 2 for 12, and 3 or more for none. Phonetic MAM's count, which has a vocal sheva and a hataf as syllables, is 0 for 209,262, 1 for 53,594 and 2 for 464. (Checked: no `jta` syllable holds both a non-syllable marker and a vowel letter, so the two counts differ in nothing but those syllables.) Measured 2026-09-09 by `.novc/mas_c_scan.py`, which raises if any of these figures moves.

The 12 fall into three groups (Ben's classification, 2026-09-09):

1. Six with a pataḥ or a segol under a guttural standing where a sheva would, all of them stress retraction before a following chanted word stressed on its first syllable.
2. Four furtive-pataḥ cases, the same stress retraction.
3. Two readings of a deḥi as the stress, and the two are not alike. At Psalms 4:3 MAM writes one deḥi, at the chanted word's start, and nothing marks a stress elsewhere, so Phonetic MAM's stress on that first syllable rests on the prepositive mark alone. At Psalms 71:9 MAM's source is a `מ:דחי` call, whose second parameter carries a second deḥi on the very letter Phonetic MAM stresses, so there Phonetic MAM is following a stress that MAM's source itself marks. `py/explicit_xataf/extract.py` names `מ:דחי` and `מ:צינור` the stress-variant templates and takes parameter 1, "since the second arg is the same word with different stress"; MAM-simple renders parameter 1 throughout, so MAM-simple carries no deḥi stress helper anywhere. That is a policy of MAM-simple and not a defect at this verse, and MAM-simple is therefore not the text to consult about a deḥi stress helper (Ben Denckla, 2026-09-09). This file said of both verses that they "look like a Phonetic MAM defect" until that day.

## The scripts and commands that regenerate every table and count

The two regeneration scripts are gitignored throwaway scripts, written 2026-09-09 in the worktree `C:/Users/BenDe/GitRepos/MAM-basics/.claude/worktrees/zen-babbage-2d6583` and revised there the same day, then copied on 2026-09-10 into the primary clone's `.novc/`, where the second was extended to fill the Koren column. Run them from the primary clone's root, `C:/Users/BenDe/GitRepos/MAM-basics`; the worktree's copies are the older ones. The first needs `MAM-private` beside the primary clone and sets `REPOS_ROOT=C:/Users/BenDe/GitRepos` itself if it is unset. To record a new Koren reading, add it to `in/meteg_after_silluq_koren_readings.json` and re-run the second script alone: the analysis JSON does not change.

1. `.novc/mas_c_scan.py` — reads the tracked FOI JSON, MAM-parsed plus and Phonetic MAM; recounts the groups; runs the Phonetic MAM census of syllables after the stress and lifts the twelve two-syllable cases; builds the exact and suffix twin indexes over all atoms of MAM; reads Phonetic MAM's sheva resolutions, for the candidates and for the base rate; grades and ranks every candidate; writes `.novc/mas_c_analysis.json` and the labelled report `.novc/mas_c_report.txt` (which also lists every minority twin, the per-accent Phonetic MAM agreement table and the base rate by vowel, with examples). It raises if 1 Kings 7:37 is not in class A or is not "long vowel + silent sheva", if the census differs from the figures above, if the twelve are not the twelve named above, or if the count of shevas with a circle or a varika differs from the `jta` field's count of vocal-sheva syllables.

```powershell

```

2. `.novc/mas_c_write_doc.py` — writes this file from that JSON, lifting every Hebrew form from it and filling the Koren column from `in/meteg_after_silluq_koren_readings.json`, then verifies the file with `uni_denorm.has_std_mark_order`, checks that every run of Hebrew in it is a lifted form, and checks that no line opens on a Hebrew character.

## What this file does not claim

1. It does not claim, from its counts alone, that any candidate other than 1 Kings 7:37 has a meteg after the silluq. It orders a lookup; Koren decides, and the section "Koren readings so far" records what Koren has decided.
2. It does not claim that twin evidence sees a pausal stress shift. A mid-verse twin is usually a context form, and only the atnach twins are in a pausal position; a candidate whose pausal form is stressed differently from its context form, and which has no atnach twin, is graded on its context forms.
3. It does not rest on any manuscript reading, and it makes no claim about Tiberian Hebrew: every count is MAM's, the syllable basis and the sheva resolutions are measurements of Phonetic MAM, and Phonetic MAM is MAM.
