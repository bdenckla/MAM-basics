## Assessment of the argument

The argument establishes substantial unfixed work at `f4d81285`. The stale receipts and plans,
instruction losses and contradictions, retirement-policy overreach, generated-page defects,
consumer-documentation gaps, public research-record inconsistencies and closed-dispatch defect
all survive this check. No numbered finding is rejected in full, and no additional endpoint
finding strong enough to add emerged from the omission search.

The counter-argument narrows several classifications and consequences. A policy or provenance
gap is not automatically a mechanically violated rule; an editorial normalization question is
not automatically a defect; a supported override is not forbidden merely because normal linked
worktrees do not need it; and a dead error field does not prove that every exception is swallowed.
The reconciliation appended to turn 01 records all 35 findings. "Confirmed" there means that a
claim survives checking, not that remediation occurred.
