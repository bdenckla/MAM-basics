## The two pages, measured at 22daa1f9

- **`gh-pages/stress-helpers/index.html`**, "Stress helpers in MAM, Koren, and Simanim", is
  generated by `py/author_site/stress_helper_comparison.py`, which `py/main_authored.py gen-site`
  runs, from `in/stress-helpers/common.json`, `koren.json` and `simanim-tanakh.json`. Its 37 rows
  each show MAM's form above the printed edition's form, the printed page and a scan crop, under
  a filter by outcome and edition. It compares Koren with MAM's printed-tradition Decalogues and
  the Simanim Tanakh with the manuscript-tradition ones.
- **`gh-pages/stress-helpers/results.html`**, "Stress-helper results in MAM, Koren, and the
  Simanim Tanakh", was written by hand in Codex's clone and added by `60ea02e6`. It reports every
  difference at 25,669 MAM readings with pashta, segolta, telisha gedolah, telisha qetanna or
  zarqa in prose verses, comparing both editions with the manuscript-tradition Decalogues, then
  adds a table against the printed tradition. Each row has MAM's form and one sentence per
  edition, with no printed form, page or crop.

## Decisions, recorded 2026-11-17

Ben accepted every recommendation on the decision page: "I accept all of your recommendations on
the decision page, by the way."

1. **1B: follow index.html's strand rule throughout.** Besides the 36 repeated rows, drop the
   three Koren rows that differ only from the manuscript tradition, which Koren does not follow:
   Exod 20:2 atom 1, Deut 5:6 atom 1 and Deut 5:13 atom 14, all תחתון. Drop the printed-tradition
   table's Simanim Tanakh side: five of its six rows agree with the manuscript tradition, and the
   sixth, the qadma at Deut 5:13 atom 1, differs from both and is in the main table already. Keep
   Koren's three grouping differences at atom 12 of Exod 20:2, Exod 20:9 and Deut 5:6, which
   differ from both traditions, with one sentence saying that Koren otherwise agrees with the
   printed tradition at all 68 of its readings with the five accents. The census table by edition
   changes for Koren only. Derived from results.html's own Decalogue figures (the printed
   tradition's 68 readings replace the manuscript tradition's 66, at which Koren had three accent
   differences), Koren has 25,671 readings, 25,586 with the same accent and helper and 44 accent
   differences, its other counts unchanged. Stage 4 recounts these from the census material. The
   census table by accent describes MAM's text with the manuscript-tradition Decalogues and stays.
2. **2A: generate results.html.** Move its kept content once, by script, into a new tracked
   input, `in/stress-helpers/results.json`, in the record shape of index.html's inputs, and
   extend `stress_helper_comparison.py` to render results.html from it with index.html's
   stylesheet, table, filter, search and Hebrew-size controls. No Hebrew is retyped.
3. **3B if the census material records each edition's form and page, otherwise 3A.** Under 3B a
   kept row shows each edition's form under MAM's, and its page, copied by script from the census
   material; under 3A, MAM's form and the sentences only. Crops wait for the merge into
   index.html, whose generator requires them.
4. **4A: the spelling-only rows become a separate collapsed table** below the differences table,
   in the same row form, so that the filter and its count cover only accent and helper
   differences.
5. **5A: say "further".** The page's title and heading become "Further stress-helper results in
   MAM, Koren, and the Simanim Tanakh". `common.json`'s link from index.html becomes "The census
   of five accents, and what it found beyond this table, are on a separate page." with the link
   text "Further results".

## Stages

**Stage 1: build `in/stress-helpers/results.json`.** Write a scratch script, in a gitignored
directory, that reads results.html at the plan's commit and writes the JSON. It copies every
Hebrew form by script and raises unless `mb_cmn.uni_denorm.has_std_mark_order` accepts it; it
drops rows by the rule and Decision 1; and it raises if any count differs from the lists above.
Each record carries index.html's fields, `book` (a bk39id), `chapter`, `verse`, `mam`, `category`
and `verse_href`, plus `atom`, the strand where results.html gives one, and each edition's
sentence. Build `verse_href` the way the existing records' links to MAM-with-doc are built,
never by hand. The outcome labels follow index.html's: a helper only in print is `extra-helper`,
"SH in ... only"; a different accent is `different-accent`; a different grouping needs a new
category, "Different grouping". One of the fourteen helpers only in print, at Ezek 16:61, sits
in a final syllable ending in two consonants with sheva, like index.html's thirteen "redundant
SH" rows; it takes `extra-helper` with the other thirteen, and whether it joins the redundant
rows is a question for the merge. The census figures and the kept prose go in the same file:
the scope and definitions, the counts by accent and by edition, Ezek 31:12's maqaf exchange,
the 14 helpers only in print, MAM's spelling alternatives, Gen 35:22, and, under 1B, the
sentence on Koren and the printed tradition. Drop the prose that restates index.html: the two
Wikisource revisions, and the counts of 12 and 13 helpers on lone pashta, which are index.html's
thirteen cases plus Deut 33:21.

**Stage 2: printed forms and pages (3B).** Read the census material. If it records each
edition's form and page for the kept rows, add them to the records by script, checking mark
order as in Stage 1; otherwise record in this plan that 3A applies and why.

**Stage 3: the generator.** Extend `stress_helper_comparison.py` to render results.html from
results.json, writing it beside index.html, with the generated-file comment. Reuse the reading,
reference and difference cells and `_romanize`. Extend the romanized names with the ones
results.html uses that index.html does not, tipeḥa, zaqef qatan and maqaf, from the `ROM_*`
constants in `py/accgram/printed_decalogue_strands.py`; render strand names from its `TAHTON` and
`ELYON` in a `<bdi>` without `lang`; and render pointed Hebrew in prose with `lang="hbo"`, on
both pages. Allow a results.json record without printed form, page or crop, and keep index.html's
check that its own records have crops. `controls.js` must work on a page without the "Show
images" button. Dispatch on every category and raise on an unknown one. Check that each census
table's rows sum to its total and raise otherwise. Apply Decision 5 to the title and to
`common.json`, and update `py/main_authored.py`'s docstring for `gen-site`, which names the pages
it writes.

**Stage 4: Koren's recount (1B).** Recount Koren's figures from the census material with the
printed-tradition Decalogues, and put the recount in results.json. If it differs from the
derived figures in Decision 1, say so, with both, in the commit message.
