The appendix’s completion date — 7 Tevet 5774 or 10 December 2014?

The Hebrew and Gregorian dates printed for the end of a round of corrections fall in different years. This is the Introduction appendix’s account of work on the edition, especially the doc-notes. It is a date-label comparison; it does not establish when that work actually ended. 7 Tevet 5774 corresponds to 10 December 2013. Conversely, 10 December 2014 corresponds to 18 Kislev 5775. The local calendar conversions round-trip, so the stated pair cannot name one date. Use the historical evidence to decide which date is intended, then make the other calendar date agree. Do not choose the event date from this mismatch alone.
