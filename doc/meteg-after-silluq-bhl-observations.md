# BHL observations on seven meteg-after-silluq cases

Ben reported the following observations on 2026-09-23 from *Biblia Hebraica
Leningradensia* (BHL). The source was his local note
`.novc/meteg after silluq - BHL.txt`; the observations are recorded here so they
do not depend on that untracked file. The BHL pages were not independently
inspected for this record. The scan filenames below are confirmed by
`in/scan-pages/bhl.json`; `doc/scan-pages.md` identifies Appendix A as
“Manuscript Variants.”

At 1 Samuel 17:5, the Appendix A entry has `(2)` after the form. Ben identifies
this as the second occurrence of that form in the verse, although the two
occurrences have different accentuation. Ben regards the split between the body
and Appendix A at this place as expected.

Ben regards BHL's silluq alone at 1 Kings 7:37 as expected because the
Leningrad Codex lacks a meteg after the silluq there. At 1 Kings 14:14, Ben
considers the absence of a meteg after the silluq in both the body and Appendix A
an oversight. Ben finds it somewhat surprising that the three Psalms forms and
the Job form with a meteg after the silluq remain in the body rather than Appendix A.
These are Ben's assessments of these seven cases, not a demonstrated general
rule for what BHL places in Appendix A. His note does not transcribe the complete
BHL pointing of the forms.
