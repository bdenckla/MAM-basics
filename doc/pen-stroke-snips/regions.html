Regions of ink

Regions drafted by an AI model, not yet reviewed by a human. Claude, an AI model, drafted every cut, seed and group on this page by a script from the strokes of the pen-stroke work, and judged every residual piece of level 1, so read the regions as a draft.

Each crop of the Leningrad Codex is drawn with its region-SVG: its ink as regions, cut where things a reader tells apart touch, each region in one group or more. The checkboxes beside each image switch that image's layers, and its menu sets its size. The profile, and what each layer means, are in doc/ink-region-representation.md; this page is generated by py/main_pen_stroke.py regions-render.

Hatched orange, outlined with dashes: a supplied mark. The crop shows little or no ink of it; it is there because the transcription or the layout implies it. Stippled teal, outlined with dots: restored ink, proposed where the image shows the ink has flaked. Neither is ever drawn in black or in a group's or a region's colour, and no other layer uses either colour.

Slate: ink of another word or line, which the crop holds but is not its subject, in every layer; in the ink by meaning it is a faint tint of slate.
