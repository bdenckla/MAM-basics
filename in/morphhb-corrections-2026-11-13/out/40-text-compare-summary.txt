== 3. Method
3.1 Atoms.  Each verse of each source becomes a list of atoms, one per space- or maqaf-delimited
unit, in text order: kind (plain, ketiv, qere), text, paseq flag.  The text drops '/', sof pasuq,
paseq and whitespace, and keeps a final maqaf.  pe, samekh and inverted-nun placeholders, and
WLC 4.2x's 'kk'/'qq' placeholders of a missing ketiv or qere, are left out.  Every element and
character is dispatched explicitly; anything else raises.  Per source:
   - morphhb: verse-level <w> in order, x-ketiv <w> as ketiv atoms, each variant note's qere
   <w> right after its ketiv <w>. A maqqef seg adds a maqaf to the <w> before it (one of
   subType x-ketiv to that ketiv alone); after a variant note, to the pair's last ketiv and
   last qere atom both, as WLC has it (52 such segs; 7 more inside rdg). A paseq seg flags the
   <w> before it; after a variant note, the pair's qere, as WLC has it (14 such segs; 5 more
   inside rdg). Note text is not text: a WLC note (n, no type) is kept as a note letter on the
   atom; KJV-versification notes are skipped; alternative notes and exegesis notes contribute
   no atoms and are used by the classification.
   - WLC 4.18 and UXLC 2.6: w, k and q elements; a paseq ends the atom after a space (UXLC 2.6
   once without one, 2 Samuel 15:12); x notes kept as note letters. UXLC 2.6 has one atom with
   a maqaf inside (Psalms 67:2), allowed for display.
   - WLC 4.20 and 4.22: '*' ketiv, '**' qere; a paseq is attached to the atom's end.
   - MAM: the lint's ketiv side (plain and ketiv atoms) and qere side (plain and qere atoms)
   separately; a standalone paseq atom flags the atom before it.
3.2 Keys.  fkey(text) (mhb_common): every mark kept, '/' and CGJ dropped, each letter's marks
sorted, so mark order never matters.  An atom's comparison tuple is (kind, fkey, paseq).  skey
is the Israel Bible review's key (accents, meteg, rafeh, paseq, sof pasuq, the upper and lower
dots and invisible marks stripped; qamats qatan read as qamats, holam haser for vav as holam).
letters() keeps the letters alone, final forms distinct.
3.3 Alignments, per verse, morphhb against each WLC version.  (a) The brief's: difflib on the
lists of comparison tuples; its non-equal opcodes are the 'hunks' counted below.  (b) The
classification's: difflib on the lists of letters, so a difference in marks never shifts the
alignment (on the tuples, a repeated word, as in Deuteronomy 5:8, can align with the wrong
copy).  Atoms in equal letter blocks pair one to one; a pair whose tuples differ is a differing
pair; a non-equal block is a letter group.  Adjacent letter groups and pairs whose kinds differ
merge into one structural unit; every other differing pair is a unit of its own.  The two
alignments always agree on which verses differ (the script raises otherwise).
3.4 Classes.  Each unit gets one label, by the first test that holds (p2_text_compare.classify):
For a differing pair (same letters, same kind):
  'zwj': equal by fkey once ZERO WIDTH JOINER is dropped from both.
  'qadma': replacing some of morphhb's HEBREW ACCENT QADMA with HEBREW ACCENT PASHTA makes the
      atoms equal by fkey, the replaced qadmas sit on exactly the letters where WLC has every
      pashta but its last, and WLC has two pashtas or more.  Nothing else is changed, so the
      replacement is exactly what makes them equal.  'qadma-other': a replacement makes them
      equal without that pattern.  'qadma+zwj': both.
  'paseq': equal by fkey, the paseq flags differ ('+paseq' is added to the labels above).
  'divided-accents/pointing' or '/accents': otherwise, in a verse with a morphhb
      alternative-accent note, as skey differs or not ('+paseq' if the paseq flags differ).
  'residual/pointing' or 'residual/accents': otherwise, likewise.
For a structural unit:
  'final-form': as many atoms on each side, kinds and paseq flags pairwise equal, and each
      pair equal by fkey once every final letter is read as its non-final form.
  'kq-structure': the sets of kinds on the two sides differ, a ketiv or qere among them.
  'word-division': both sides non-empty, the same set of kinds, the same letters in the same
      order split into different atoms ('+marks' if the concatenations differ by fkey).
  'divided-accents/letters' in a verse with an alternative note, else 'residual/letters'.
The task's classes group the labels: qadma-for-first-pashta = qadma (and qadma+zwj, qadma+paseq);
Decalogue/divided accents = divided-accents/*; mark encoding only = zwj and final-form; word
division = word-division*; ketiv/qere representation = kq-structure; paseq = paseq; residual =
residual/* and qadma-other.  A residual is split into letters, pointing (skey differs) and
accents only (skey equal).
3.5 Every unit of the closest version is listed in 43-text-class-lists.txt; every residual, and
every unit of the rarer labels, with WLC 4.18's, 4.20's, 4.22's, UXLC 2.6's and MAM's atoms at
the same place (aligned by letters with both morphhb's and WLC's atoms) in 41-text-residuals.*.

== 8. Limitations
- WLC 4.20 and 4.22 are read through MAM-basics' own Unicode conversion of the
Michigan-Claremont files, not through tanach.us's or morphhb's; a difference that the
conversion makes (the final forms of 5.3) looks like a difference of version. WLC 4.18 is
tanach.us's Unicode, which morphhb's text resembles in encoding (final forms, ZERO WIDTH
JOINER).
- No WLC 4.20 in tanach.us's encoding, the edition morphhb most likely took (its commit 'Update
to WLC 4.20' of 2016-03-04), was available locally; so the version question rests on content
differences between 4.18, 4.20 and 4.22, not on byte identity.
- The comparison ignores morpheme division ('/'), sof pasuq, the lemma and morph attributes,
and the text of notes (WLC note letters are compared separately per verse, as multisets, not by
position).
- The classes are defined by tests on the data, not by morphhb's intent: a 'divided-accents'
unit is any difference in a verse with an alternative note; the union test of 5.2 is what shows
that the division alone accounts for most of them.
- difflib alignments: the letters alignment pairs atoms with identical letters; a unit whose
letters also changed (none against WLC 4.20) would be a group, classified by the structural
tests.
- MAM is shown only at the listed places, read on its ketiv side for plain and ketiv atoms and
its qere side for qere atoms, dual cantillation in MAM-simple's cant-combined strand; MAM's
kind is not compared, and its ketiv-side atoms are shown without [k].
- UXLC 2.6 and MAM are display only: they are not used to classify anything.
