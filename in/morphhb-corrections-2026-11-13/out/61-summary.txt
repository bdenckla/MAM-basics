== 3. What each method cannot find
1. Method 1 (the al/el census) sees only words whose last morpheme is alef-lamed.  Within them it finds every disagreement between vowel, lemma and morph, whether or not MAM differs, so it also finds morphhb's own slips (Jeremiah 51:3, Genesis 37:22), which are not corrections.  It cannot see a correction at any other word.
2. Method 2 (the general test) compares morphhb's form with MAM's by the review key, which strips accents, meteg, rafeh and the dots.  It cannot find: (a) a correction where WLC and MAM agree, since there is no pair (the exchanged lemmas of Jeremiah 51:3 are invisible to it); (b) a correction carried by a mark the key strips, such as an accent whose place distinguishes two forms; (c) a correction of an analysis that both forms share, where none is possible anyway; (d) a correction whose own form is rare (fewer than five other occurrences: 865 pairs), except through its back-off tests and Claude's reading of the rare lists; back-off B, which draws on MAM's text, flagged nothing, and back-off A is mostly noise; (e) a correction where MAM's form does not occur in WLC's text (49 pairs with a common own form and 502 rare ones are untestable by frequency; Claude read the rare ones whose mark difference could change an analysis, list 57); (f) a correction that morphhb makes often enough to raise its share among its own form's occurrences above the threshold (the grid in 59-threshold-sensitivity.txt shows no further flag up to 30%); (g) a correction where the letters differ, except through the supplement, which covers one-to-one blocks only (1,017 pairs), not word division or ketiv/qere structure; (h) a correction of a ketiv against WLC's own qere: the test compares a ketiv with MAM's ketiv side, where MAM often has no ketiv or points it.
3. Method 3 (WLC's notes) sees only words that WLC 4.20 annotates, as morphhb carries the notes.  Of the corrections, Exodus 10:28, Joshua 22:19, Jeremiah 47:6 and Leviticus 4:35 carry no note in morphhb (WLC 4.22's notes 1 at the first three came after morphhb's text), so this method cannot find them; within annotated words it inherits method 2's limits.
