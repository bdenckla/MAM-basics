== 1. Method
Writes <out_dir>/60-wlc-notes.txt and .json.  Run after p2_lemma_general.py, whose pairs, flags
and verdicts it reads from 51-general-test.json.

Every WLC note in morphhb is a <note> with an n attribute (the note letter) and no type.  Its
chanted word is what the note follows: the last <w> before it (skipping other notes, sof pasuq and
paseq segs), or, when a variant note comes last, that ketiv/qere pair (its ketiv words and its qere
words); with every <w> joined to them by maqaf before them, on each side.  Each word of the chanted
word is looked up in this folder's alignment of morphhb with MAM (p2_lemma_lib.align_all), on each
side it is on: "same" if it is aligned one to one with a MAM atom of the same form key, "skey" if
with a MAM atom of the same letters and another form key, "letters" if it is in a block whose
letters differ.  A chanted word differs from MAM if any of its words is "skey" or "letters".  The
general test's results (flagged, flagged by a back-off test, and Claude's verdicts) are attached
by morphhb id.
