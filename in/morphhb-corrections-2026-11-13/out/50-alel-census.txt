== 1. Counts

  of them aligned one to one with a MAM atom of the same letters: 4924; not: 6
mismatches: 17 (the known five among them: 5)
  by Claude's class: anomaly 1, correction 5, defensible 8, slip 3
    anomaly: an anomaly read as the intended word: WLC's form is no regular word or form in its place (often under a WLC note), and the analysis is that of the word both transcriptions mean, which MAM's form spells regularly; Part 1's class c
    correction: a correction: WLC's form, as written, is a regular spelling of another word or of another inflection, and the lemma or the morph analyses MAM's reading instead
    defensible: a homograph, or an analysis that WLC's form admits as written
    slip: an inconsistency of morphhb's own: the lemma or morph fits neither WLC's form nor MAM's, and the two agree
alef-lamed words whose alef has another vowel in MAM's atom: 6
