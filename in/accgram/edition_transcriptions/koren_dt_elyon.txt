# (koren_ex_elyon, p. 38) was the first Koren page to diverge: it splits two maqaf compounds the

# habit:
#   * לא יהיה לך (5:7): Koren has לֹ֣א־יִֽהְיֶ֣ה לְךָ֩ -- a maqaf joining לא to יהיה, a munax on each of
#     those two atoms, then a space and לך with telisha qetana.  ws/dt/elyon/printed has לא
#     (munax) and the compound יהיה־לך (telisha qetana), with no accent on יהיה.  So Koren
#     separates יהיה from לך here as on its Exodus page, though into two chanted words rather
#     than three.  No strand has what this page has.  Only the four elyon strands have the
#     compound יהיה־לך, and none of them splits it.  The four taxton strands have this page's
#     grouping -- לא־יהיה, then לך alone -- but with a merkha on יהיה and a tevir on לך,
#     where this page has the elyon's munax on לא and telisha qetana on לך, and a munax on יהיה
#     that no strand has.
#   * אַתָּה וּבִנְךָ־וּבִתֶּךָ (Shabbat): Koren joins ובנך־ובתך (munax), as ws/dt/elyon/printed has it.
#     Note the corroboration is REVERSED from Exodus: there the two ex/taxton strands split ובנך,
#     which is what corroborated the koren_ex_elyon split; in DEUTERONOMY all four dt strands
#     (elyon+taxton, manuscript+printed) JOIN ובנך־ובתך, so no strand splits it and a split would
#     have been Koren-alone and uncorroborated.  Koren joins it -- no divergence either way.

# Koren does NOT distinguish narrow-sense paseq from legarmeh (Ben, primary observation about the
# physical book, recorded nowhere else -- hence the pasoleg_kinds header field).  So all seven
# vertical strokes here, on פסל בשמים במים צוך שבת היית and למען, are written "[pasoleg]": a
# pasoleg stands here, this edition does not say which kind.  Writing "mun_leg" or "[paseq]"
# would assert something the book does not.  This is more strokes than any Koren page so far.
#
# Conventions in force for this file:
#   * One token per ACCENT.  Normally that is one per chanted word, a maqaf compound being one
#     chanted word; the exception is a chanted word bearing more than one accent.  Two occur
#     here, the compounds לא־יהיה (line 2) and לא־תעשה (line 12), each with a munax on each atom.
#   * "-" is a maqaf binding two ACCENTS into one chanted word ("mun-mun" on לא־יהיה, line 2, and
#     on לא־תעשה, line 12), "+" its simple-word counterpart, "_" binds two MARKS into ONE accent
#     ("mun_leg").  The maqaf occurs twice (lines 2 and 12); the other two do not.
#   * A postpositive/prepositive accent written twice on one word -- once at its fixed edge and
#     once as a stress helper -- is ONE accent, so ONE token.
#   * meteg is NOT transcribed: it is not a first-class mark of the accent system.
#   * A pasoleg is not an accent and is not a token; it is recorded as the aside "[pasoleg]"
#     after the token it follows.  The parser drops bracketed asides.
#
