Hello Chris and Ben,

Below are 24 proposed suggested corrections and updates for the UXLC based on my recent analysis of the Leningrad Codex (L) images, along with comparisons to BHS and Dotan.

JEREMIAH

Jer 3:24.2 (אָֽכְלָ֛ה)
Image: 492_Jer_2.11b-2.37a | Col. 1 middle
Correction: Replace Meteg with Merekha.
Corrected Text: אָ֥כְלָ֛ה
Note: L has a Merekha under Aleph.

Jer 8:9.9 (וְחָכְמַֽת־)
Image: 499_Jer_7.24b-8.9a | Col. 3 bottom
Correction: Remove Maqqef and replace Meteg with Merekha OR remove Maqqef and add note that other editions read as Merekha.
Corrected Text: וְחׇכְמַ֥ת
Note: L does not have a Maqqef at the end of this word. BHS reads as Meteg and Dotan reads as Merekha.

Jer 10:3.11 (יְדֵ֥י־)
Image: 501_Jer_9.9b-10.9a | Col. 3 middle
Correction: Change Merkha to Meteg.
Corrected Text: יְדֵֽי־
Note: L has a Meteg under Dalet. BHS and UXLC read as Merekha. Dotan reads as Meteg.

Jer 12:10.8 (נָֽתְנ֛וּ)
Image: 504_Jer_12.6b-13.13a | Col. 1 middle
Correction: Replace Meteg with Merekha.
Corrected Text: נָ֥תְנ֛וּ
Note: L has a Merekha under the first Nun.

Jer 28:9.7 (הַנָּבִ֗יא)
Image: 521_Jer_28.4b-29.7 | Col. 1 middle
Correction: Change Revia to Zaqef Qatan.
Corrected Text: הַנָּבִ֔יא
Note: L is damaged but there appears to be two dots above Beit rather than just one.

Jer 46:4.6 (בְּכ֥וֹבָעִ֑ים)
Image: 542_Jer_46.2b-46.28a | Col. 1 middle
Correction: Change Merkha to Meteg.
Corrected Text: בְּכֽוֹבָעִ֑ים
Note: L has a Meteg under Kaf. BHS and UXLC read as Merekha. Dotan reads as Meteg.

EZEKIEL

Ezek 1:4.12 (וְנֹ֥גַֽהּ)
Image: 552_Jer_52.26b-Eze_1.16a | Col. 2 middle
Correction: Add note regarding appearance of accent.
Corrected Text: וְנֹ֥גַֽהּ
Note: BHS, Dotan, and UXLC all read with Merekha. Add a note that the image in L resembles a Meteg but is read as a Merekha.

Ezek 8:6.12 (בֵּֽית־יִשְׂרָאֵ֣ל ׀)
Image: 558_Eze_7.20b-8.16a | Col. 2 middle
Correction: Remove Maqqef and replace Meteg with Merekha OR remove Maqqef and add note that other editions read as Merekha.
Corrected Text: בֵּ֥ית יִשְׂרָאֵ֣ל ׀
Note: L does not have a Maqqef. BHS reads as Meteg and Dotan reads as Merekha. This phrase is noted in the Masorah as it is one of the rare instances where two Merekhas serve Legarmeh.

Ezek 8:15.9 (תּוֹעֵב֥וֹת)
Image: 558_Eze_7.20b-8.16a | Col. 3 bottom
Correction: Replace Merekha with Meteg OR note that the accent looks like a Meteg.
Corrected Text: תּוֹעֵב֖וֹת
Note: L has what looks like a Meteg under the Beit. This resembles a Tifcha more than a Merekha but there is a Tifcha on the next word. This is the classic switch of Tifcha and Merekha seen throughout L when compared to other MSS.

Ezek 11:1.29 (שָׂרֵ֖י)
Image: 560_Eze_10.7b-11.10a | Col. 3 top
Correction: Replace Tifcha with Merekha OR add note that the accent looks like a Meteg.
Corrected Text: שָׂרֵ֥י
Note: BHS, Dotan, and UXLC read with Tifcha. However, the image of L does not look like a Tifcha but resembles a Meteg, which in many cases was the same shape for Merkha.

Ezek 16:12.1 (וָאֶתֵּ֥ן נֶ֙זֶם֙)
Image: 565_Eze_15.1-16.20 | Col. 2 bottom
Correction: Add a Maqqef between words and change Merekha to Meteg.
Corrected Text: וָ֥אֶתֵּֽן־נֶ֙זֶם֙
Note: L has a Maqqef between these words and there is a Meteg under the Tav.

Ezek 23:11.8 (וְאֶת־תַּ֨זְנוּתֶ֔יהָ)
Image: 575_Eze_22.25b-23.21 | Col. 2 bottom
Correction: Remove Azla from above Tav OR add note.
Corrected Text: וְאֶת־תַּזְנוּתֶ֔יהָ
Note: The Azla and Zaqef Qatan on a word is common in L but there are at least 27 places where the Aleppo has both accents on a word while L only has the Zaqef Qatan. The faint markings above Tav are questionable.

Ezek 32:21.9 (יָֽרְד֛וּ)
Image: 585_Eze_32.1-32.25a | Col. 3 bottom
Correction: Add note regarding appearance of accent.
Corrected Text: יָ֥רְד֛וּ
Note: BHS and UXLC read as Meteg. Dotan reads as Merekha. Add a note that the image in L resembles a Meteg but is read as a Merekha by some.

Ezek 33:28.9 (וְשָֽׁמְמ֛וּ)
Image: 587_Eze_33.13b-34.4a | Col. 2 bottom
Correction: Change Meteg to Merekha.
Corrected Text: וְשָׁ֥מְמ֛וּ
Note: L has a Merekha under Shin.

Ezek 36:3.18 (וַתֵּֽעֲל֛וּ)
Image: 589_Eze_34.26b-36.4a | Col. 3 bottom
Correction: Add note regarding appearance of accent.
Corrected Text: וַתֵּ֥עֲל֛וּ
Note: BHS and UXLC read as Meteg. Dotan reads as Merekha. Add a note that the image in L resembles a Meteg but is read as a Merekha by some.

Ezek 44:6.14 (תּוֹעֲבֽוֹתֵיכֶ֖ם)
