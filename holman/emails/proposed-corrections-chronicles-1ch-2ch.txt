Dear Chris and Ben,

Please review the following suggested corrections and notes regarding the UXLC text and image locations for the book of Chronicles.

Many of these items address the visual identification of Merekha versus meteg in the Leningrad Codex (L) compared to modern editions like Dotan, BHS, and Ginsburg.

Suggested Corrections:

1. 1Ch 2:46.4 (יָֽלְדָ֛ה)
Image Location: 656_1Ch_2.23b-3.1 | Col. 2 bottom
Correction: Add a note regarding other MSS reading as Merekha, or change Meteg to Merekha and add a note.
Target Text: יָֽלְדָ֛ה OR יָ֥לְדָ֛ה

Note: Dotan has Merekha. Bomberg, Ginsburg, and BHS have Meteg.

2. 1Ch 4:28.1 (וַיֵּֽשְׁב֛וּ)
Image Location: 658_1Ch_4.12b-4.41a | Col. 2 bottom
Correction: Add a note regarding other MSS reading as Merekha, or change meteg to Merekha and add a note.
Target Text: וַיֵּֽשְׁב֛וּ OR וַיֵּ֥שְׁב֛וּ

Note: Dotan has Merekha. Bomberg and BHS have Meteg. Ginsburg has no mark under yod.

3. 1Ch 5:11.4 (יָֽשְׁב֛וּ)
