# Holman review and correction data in MAM-basics

This product directory holds the data, rendered reports, and supporting assets derived from three bodies of work that Daniel Holman sent Ben Denckla:

- ketiv/qere review data
- suggested UXLC corrections
- suggested MAM corrections

The ketiv/qere review and the MAM correction proposals share the findings report at [`../gh-pages/holman/table_data_findings.html`](../gh-pages/holman/table_data_findings.html). The UXLC correction report is [`../gh-pages/holman/uxlc_corrections.html`](../gh-pages/holman/uxlc_corrections.html). Both reports are published under [bdenckla.github.io/MAM-basics/holman/](https://bdenckla.github.io/MAM-basics/holman/).

MAM-basics owns the pipeline entry points and the tests. `docs-not-served/`, `emails/`, `data/`, `out/`, `assets/`, and `io/` hold the product artifacts that the pipeline reads or writes. The raw email files remain untracked because the public tracked derivatives remove email addresses.

For the two source-specific research records, see [`doc/holman-manuscript-citations.md`](doc/holman-manuscript-citations.md) and [`doc/uxlc-email-count-disagreements.md`](doc/uxlc-email-count-disagreements.md).
