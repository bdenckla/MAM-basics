# Things Noticed in cam1753

- One of the supposed double-pe breaks is more like a double-samekh. Not sure where — chapter 5 or so?
- Job 10:14: לא has מונח in MAM; has מקף rather than מונח in cam1753.
- Job 19:21: כי has מקף in cam1753.
- Job 21:28: אהל no פסק.
- Job 32:11: last word seems to end in a final mem not a final nun, i.e. seems to be מלים in cam1753.
- Job 39:18: followed by a kind of extraordinary pair of horizontal lines, the top one being squiggly. See also Page 0084A, col 2, line 16.
- Job 40:21: first word תחת seems to be missing end-of-line מקף.
- **Two different book-name annotations, and telling them apart matters.** Both read off the
  images by Ben, 2026-08-04.
  - **איכה**, no preceding ספר, above Lam 1:1 on leaf 0105A col 2. This one **does** take up
    body-column space, and marks the start of the book.
  - **ספר איכה**, centered, one instance above leaf 0105B col 2 — whose text starts at
    Lam 1:17, mid-book. This one appears **sporadically** through the book, on no rule Ben
    could determine, and takes **no** body-column space wherever it appears. **What governs
    it is open**, and an instance says nothing about where a book begins.
- **Lamentations begins on leaf 0105A col 2, nineteen lines up from the bottom** (line 8 of
  26), with Ecclesiastes ending in the same column above the איכה heading. Spread n109,
  fourth column of the spread counting RTL. Ben, 2026-08-04.
- Every Lamentations column reading is in `cam1753-page-index.json`, each with the text as
  Ben read it off the image as well as the MAM text it matches. Those entries are what to
  build on; a verse's page can be interpolated between them without anything further.
