Daniel Holman's observations on the Hebrew Bible text

Two reports, covering three bodies of work Daniel Holman has sent Ben Denckla.

  • Suggestions for the UXLC Mostly places where Holman reads the Leningrad Codex images differently from the UXLC text, with the rest asking only for an apparatus note; proposed to the UXLC's editor a book at a time.
  • Holman MAM suggestions Holman's review of qere and ketiv readings in the Aleppo and Leningrad codices, and what MAM might take from it; together with his suggested corrections to MAM itself, which are about a meteg or about where an accent sits. One page, filterable by which of the two a record is.
