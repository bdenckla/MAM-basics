The general test and WLC's notes

A general test of lemmas and morphs flags the review's five corrections and two of the four others; Claude's reading of pairs that no test flags found the other two, and WLC's notes point to no further correction. The test aligned morphhb's atoms with MAM's by letters, ketiv side with ketiv side and qere side with qere side, and took the 7,757 one-to-one pairs whose letters agree and whose forms differ by the review's key; 6,466 of them differ only by a ḥolam in MAM's form, 6,459 of those in the Tetragrammaton. A word is flagged when its lemma, its morph or both are common among the other occurrences of MAM's form (at least 50%) and rare among those of its own form (at most 10% of at least 5).

  1. Of the 6,892 pairs testable so, 8 are flagged: the five corrections, an anomaly at Daniel 3:15 and two that are noise, at Exodus 12:27 and 1 Chronicles 29:16; a looser grid of thresholds flags nothing more. The test flags all five corrections and not Judges 19:4.
  2. Of the 865 pairs whose own form is rarer, a back-off test flags 54, among which Genesis 39:19 is the one correction.
  3. Of the 1,017 pairs whose letters differ, 27 are flagged, among which Hosea 13:15 is the one correction.
  4. Claude's reading of 13 pairs that no test flags found Deuteronomy 22:29 and Leviticus 4:35.

What these methods cannot find

Six kinds of correction are beyond these methods:

  1. A correction where WLC and MAM agree, or where the lemma and morph are the same for both forms.
  2. A correction carried by marks that the review's key strips, such as accents.
  3. A correction at a word whose own form is too rare for the general test, beyond what the back-off test and Claude's reading of 13 pairs caught, and at the 49 pairs whose MAM form never occurs in morphhb.
  4. A letter difference outside a one-to-one block of the alignment.
  5. A ketiv analysed by its qere: at Isaiah 52:5 the ketiv carries the qere's lemma, while 205 other ketiv words carry their own.
  6. Anything about the Leningrad Codex itself: no image was consulted.

Where this page departs from the report

Where the report's data show its README to be wrong or imprecise, this page says what the data show. Each place:

  1. The Decalogues' vowels and maqaf. The report's README, under Part 2: all other such corrections in morphhb, The answer, item 4, says: Changes of letters, vowels or maqaf are confined to three words of the Decalogues, Daniel 2:39's ketiv and qere and the 12 words that morphhb divides for exegesis.
