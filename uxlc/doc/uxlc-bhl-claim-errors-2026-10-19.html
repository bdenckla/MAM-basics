Errors in BHL claims in UXLC change-record notes

19 October 2026

This copy, tracked in MAM-basics, omits the ten BHLA scan crops shown in the copy that Ben Denckla sent to Chris Kimball, UXLC's editor, on 19 October 2026. uxlc-bhl-claim-errors-2026-10-19.json gives each crop's recipe and the sent files' SHA-256 hashes.

Many UXLC change records have a note saying what BHL (BHL body), or its Appendix A (BHLA), has at the word being changed. In some cases these claims are at odds with what BHL or BHLA actually has; those cases are listed below.

BHLA has an entry for the word

Each of these notes says that BHLA has no entry, for the word or for the verse:

  • “BHL Appendix A has no entry for this word.”
  • “BHL Appendix A has no entries for this word.”
  • “BHL Appendix A has no note on this word in the verse.”
  • “BHLA has no entry for this verse.”

But in truth we find that BHLA does have an entry for the word in question; it is shown above the change record's UXLC word. Records 2026.02.05-22, 2026.08.05-8 and 2026.08.05-115 leave UXLC with BHLA's word, but none has the analysis tag that says so; each record's page instead says, by default, “The UXLC does not agree with either BHL text.”

  1. Prov 12:10.7: our transcription of BHLA has the silluq on the zayin, after its qamats, but BHLA's marks there are cramped, so that assignment is uncertain. Even so, BHLA as we transcribe it and “UXLC after” agree on the main point: both have the silluq between the qamats and the ḥiriq. What is unclear, in the manuscript as in BHLA, is whether the silluq is on the resh, before its ḥiriq, or on the zayin, after its qamats. “UXLC after” has it on the resh, which is in some ways the more charitable interpretation of the manuscript: it posits only an “early silluq”, not a change to the word's stress.
  2. Job 10:15.12: on its first yod, UXLC's word after the change has three characters, in this order: HEBREW ACCENT MERKHA (U+05A5), ZERO WIDTH JOINER (U+200D) and HEBREW POINT HIRIQ (U+05B4). UXLC's joiner keeps the merkha displayed before the ḥiriq, as the record's note describes the manuscript: “a sloping line followed by a hiriq.” Side by side, the merkha's stroke and the ḥiriq's dot look like a tevir, but the word has no HEBREW ACCENT TEVIR (U+059B).
  3. Mic 1:14.10: UXLC's earlier record for this word, 2022.12.12-9, correctly says “BHL Appendix A has a merkha under the kaf.”

Each of these notes says “BHLA has no entry for this verse.” BHLA has an entry for the verse, but it is for another word, so the sentence is true in spirit. We suggest replacing it with “BHLA has no entry for this word.”

Suggestion for Prov 23:26.8 (2024.04.15-35):

BHLA agrees with UXLC that there is no dagesh in the צ in Leningrad.

Nonetheless, BHL body supplies, ex nihilo, a dagesh in the צ of its pointed qere, because that is the consensus pointing of this word and BHL body is merely a Leningrad-flavored consensus edition, not a diplomatic edition of Leningrad.

UXLC does not have a dagesh in the צ of its pointed qere, because UXLC is a diplomatic edition of Leningrad, albeit one that has only unpointed ketiv and pointed qere, a somewhat awkward exception to its diplomatic stance, since of course Leningrad has only pointed ketiv and unpointed qere.

BHLA agrees with UXLC that the ḥolam ḥaser dot is on the צ in Leningrad.

Nonetheless, in the journey from ketiv to qere, BHL body allows this ḥolam ḥaser dot to travel with its letter, צ, rather than stay with its letter-position, 3. Note that in BHL body and UXLC, the under-marks, silluq and sheva, stay with their letter-positions (2 and 3) rather than traveling with their letters across the רצ to צר transposition journey that is the essence of this ketiv/qere.

BHL body allows the ḥolam ḥaser dot to travel with its letter, צ, for the same reason that it supplies a dagesh in the צ of its pointed qere. BHLA notes that the consensus pointed ketiv (the "instead of" form of this word) would have the ḥolam ḥaser dot on ר.

UXLC does not allow the ḥolam ḥaser dot to travel with its letter, צ, for the same reason that its pointed qere has no dagesh in the צ.
