--- review comment on wlc/1Kgs.xml 1161973381 by rkjtan (NONE) at 2023-04-10T18:35:49Z
The patah vowel is already explained by the fact that if the noun begins with a guttural with a hatep vowel, the preposition takes the corresponding full vowel. So, there is no need to posit the definite article here,
--- review comment on wlc/1Kgs.xml 1161980510 by jdejoode (CONTRIBUTOR) at 2023-04-10T18:44:56Z
Yes, I thought that was the logic :+1: So I've seen more of these in the same pull request, did you systematically check for cases of a number in the hundreds and whether the direct article was tagged or not?
--- review comment on wlc/1Kgs.xml 1161988842 by jdejoode (CONTRIBUTOR) at 2023-04-10T18:55:36Z
A possible reference in support is JM §35.e  which mentions a similar case where it cannot be known whether the definite article is present or not. It would indeed be safer in that case not to assume the article is there; this is also supported by other parallels.
